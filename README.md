## The entry

> Don't sign that lease on Maple Street in March 2019.
> The landlord raised the rent twice in a year, and the money you bled there is the down payment you never saved.

164 characters, two lines, each one sentence.

## Why this pair

The moment is as concrete as the judge brief asks for: a named street, a
month and a year, and a single decision — signing that lease. Line two does
not restate the warning; it traces the chain of consequences step by step:
two rent hikes in one year, money bled away month after month, and the
down payment that never got saved because of it. The voice is plain and
addressed to one person, with no wasted words and no generic life lesson.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) validates the
entry against the gate — UTF-8 text, 1–280 characters, not whitespace-only —
then writes it to `entry.txt` under `ENTRY_OUT_DIR`, `ENTRY_OUT`, or
`/output/entry.txt`.
